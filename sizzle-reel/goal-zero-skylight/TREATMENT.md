# Goal Zero Skylight: "Make Your Own Daylight"

66-second motion sizzle reel, 1920x1080, 24 fps, scored at 120 BPM so every cut, click and clunk lands on the beat (one beat is exactly 12 frames).

## The idea

Every other light in this category sells lumens. Skylight's real story is how it moves. A mast climbs from 4 to 12 feet, six petals open like a flower, and a campsite at midnight starts looking like late afternoon. So the reel plays out like a sunrise. It opens at dusk with the light fading out, follows the mechanical rise beat by beat, and drops when the petals open and 6,000 lumens hit the ground.

The world is built in real-time 3D: a matte, low-detail night landscape where the only hero material is the product and the only real color is the warm 3250K light. Props stay simple on purpose, so the eye goes to the light and the shadows it throws.

## Look

- Palette: night navy (#070B14 to #1A2233) and Skylight warm (3250K, about #FFC491). Cool moonlight on rims, warm light on everything Skylight touches.
- Type: Archivo Expanded Black for headlines (wide and rugged), JetBrains Mono for specs and data, so the reel reads like gear plus engineering.
- Film finish: 2.39:1 letterbox that opens to full frame the moment the light comes on, bloom, anamorphic flare, split-tone grade, grain.
- On-screen system: numbered spec chapters (01 / OUTPUT, 02 / HEAD...), callouts that track the 3D model, live HUD readouts for height, mode, color temperature and power.

## Score

An original temp track synthesized for this cut: dusk drone, wind and crickets, a pulse that builds under the mast locks, a riser and snare roll into the drop, then a vi-IV-I-V groove (Bm, G, D, A) through the features. It breaks down to rain for IPX4 and resolves on D major for the logo. All the sound design is timed to on-screen events (mast clunks, petal ticks, mode clicks, the Yeti plug).

## Product facts used

6,000 lumen Boost (Low 400, Med 1,350, High 3,500). Mast 4 to 12 ft. Six petals, 28 LEDs each (168 total), each pivots through 180 degrees. 3250K color temperature. 33Wh built-in battery, about 8 hours on Low. Up to 375 hours on Low with a Yeti 1500X. Lights up to 300 ft. IPX4. Integrated aluminum tripod with three ground stakes. Collapses to 44.7 in with a hard case included.
